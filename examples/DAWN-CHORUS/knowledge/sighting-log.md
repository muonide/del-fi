# DAWN-CHORUS — Salmonberry Creek Sighting Log

What has been seen at Salmonberry Creek Preserve lately: notable bird sightings and preserve conditions, newest first. The volunteer stewards update this log weekly from their own walks, visitors' !post messages and the listening station (see birdnet-station.md). For what is being detected right now, ask the node or send !data.

Last updated: September 25, 2026.

---

## September 2026

### September 24, 2026: Merlin Over the Meadow

A Merlin hunted over the Sedge Meadow at about 8:10 am, taking a sparrow in a fast, low chase before perching on the tallest snag at the Snag Overlook. Merlins are back for the winter; watch the meadow edges in the morning.

### September 23, 2026: First Varied Thrush of the Autumn

The first Varied Thrush of the season called from the Cedar Trail near footbridge 1 (a harsh "chuck", not yet singing), and the listening station detected one at 7:02 am. Expect more through October as they come down from the mountains.

### September 22, 2026: Golden-crowned Sparrows Arrive

The first four Golden-crowned Sparrows of the season fed with Song Sparrows on the Meadow Path, a sure sign of autumn. By mid-October flocks of 20 or more are usual along the brushy edges.

### September 21, 2026: Ducks Returning

Three Ring-necked Ducks and a pair of Wood Ducks were on the Beaver Pond. Hooded Mergansers and Buffleheads usually return in October. The beaver dam, damaged in the September 17 rainstorm, has been repaired by the beavers, and the pond is back to its normal level.

### September 19, 2026: Last Swainson's Thrush

The listening station recorded the soft "heep" flight calls of Swainson's Thrushes migrating overhead at 2:10 am, the last detection of the season. They will be back in mid-May.

### September 16, 2026: Pileated Woodpecker Excavation

A pair of Pileated Woodpeckers has been chopping a large new rectangular hole in a dead hemlock beside Cedar Trail footbridge 2, leaving a pile of fresh chips on the trail. Watch from the footbridge; please do not approach the tree.

### September 13, 2026: Sedge Meadow Mowed

The stewards mowed the Sedge Meadow for the year. Northern Flickers and American Robins have been feeding on the cut grass, and a Red-tailed Hawk hunts voles from the snags along the edge.

### September 6, 2026: Barred Owl Family

A Barred Owl pair and one young owl have been calling at dusk along the Cedar Trail. The young owl gives a hissing, rising begging screech. Please keep your distance and do not use lights or recordings.

---

## August 2026

### August 28, 2026: Ospreys Gone

The Osprey family has left the nest snag at the Snag Overlook for the winter; the pair raised two young this year. Swallows are gathering in flocks on the Beaver Pond snags before leaving.

### August 15, 2026: Green Heron at the Blind

A Green Heron fished from a low alder branch in front of the Beaver Pond Blind for most of the morning, a treat for visitors.
